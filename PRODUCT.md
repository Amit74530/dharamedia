# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and decision-makers at hotels, resorts, homestays, clinics, wellness centres and other service businesses who want to improve their brand and digital presence.

## Product Purpose

Dhara Media is an independent creative studio in Dehradun that helps businesses build their brand, website and social presence to grow bookings and enquiries.

## Positioning

The studio brings strategy, design and technology together, with work shaped around each business rather than a fixed package.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services include social media management, website design and development, brand identity, digital marketing, influencer marketing and content production.
- The site supports project enquiries through its form, email, phone and configured WhatsApp links.
- Preserve the existing routes, navigation, SEO metadata, enquiry flow and contact integrations.
- Do not add unverified testimonials, client names, performance statistics or portfolio claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Dhara Media name, original logo, and established forest green, orange, ivory and ink palette.
- Keep the studio's voice concise, practical and clear.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved case studies and verified outcomes are not currently published.
- Homepage stock imagery and video are illustrative, not client work; local media is licensed under the Pexels License.
- The founder is Amit Singh Karki.

## Product Principles

- Connect brand strategy, design and technology into practical work.
- Help prospective customers understand a business and take the next step.
- Shape scope around real business needs rather than assuming a standard package.
- Keep proof transparent and publish project claims only when verified and approved.
